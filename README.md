# Jukello Schedule Desktop

The appointment scheduler that runs on one PC and never asks you for another payment.

Book appointments, manage staff schedules and fill your waiting list from a single Windows machine at the front desk — no server, no IT setup, no monthly bill eating into your margins.

**Live page:** https://fiae2020.github.io/jukello-schedule-desktop/

## Why businesses switch to it

- **Waiting list that fills itself** — cancel a slot, instantly see matching clients, one click to rebook
- **Double-booking is impossible** — enforced at the database level, not just a warning you can click past
- **Staff scheduling built in** — see every team member's day at a glance
- **Guardian & minor handling** — automatic routing for clients booked by a parent or guardian
- **Runs fully offline** — your data stays on your machine, not someone else's cloud

## Pricing that doesn't punish you for staying

14-day free trial, every feature unlocked. Decide to keep it? **$249.99 once** — no subscription, no renewal, no per-seat fees. Own it forever.

## Multiple staff logging in at once? Try the web version instead

This desktop build is single-PC by design. If you need several people working simultaneously across one or more locations, the **on-premise web version** is the same product self-hosted on your own server — unlimited staff, unlimited branches, still a one-time payment.

→ **On-premise web version:** https://schedule-onpremise.jukello.com/
→ **Full product, pricing & desktop registration:** https://scheduler.jukello.com/
